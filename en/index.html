01

The decision it serves

Context

A Chinese manufacturer cannot bid in a Saudi public tender in its own name. The award is signed by a Saudi company holding the registration, the import licence and the local service obligation. That company is the one that decides what gets sold, at what price, and to which hospital.

Every Chinese manufacturer in the market is therefore attached to a local supplier — usually to exactly one. What changes your year is not how many Chinese manufacturers you meet, but which ones are currently unrepresented, or badly represented, and can be won at low cost.

We do not score distributors, and we do not rank manufacturers by quality. We report what the awards show, and where the record stops.

03

What it cannot prove

Limits

NUPCO publishes no prices. We cannot tell you what anyone charged or was paid. An award line is a signature, not a shipment: it does not tell you volume, stock or whether the item was ever delivered. 45.8% of award lines could not be assigned to a product category, so category-level statements hold only for the lines that could.

A further 10.1% of the lines we tag as Chinese-manufacturer are third-party products described as “compatible with” a Chinese instrument, not made by that manufacturer. We separate those out rather than count them as the manufacturer's record — a distinction that matters if you check the notices yourself.

Private hospitals, group purchasing organisations and markets outside Saudi Arabia are not covered. Public tender awards are one channel; they are not the whole market.
